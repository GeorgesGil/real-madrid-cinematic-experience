## Required delivery flow

For the primary implementation issue, read each relevant `SKILL.md` before acting and follow this sequence: `research`, `grill-with-docs`, `prototype`, `to-spec`, `codebase-design`, `implement`, `tdd`, and `code-review`.

Keep research and design evidence under `docs/`. Do not claim visual or performance verification without recorded evidence.
